Data policy

Breeders decide who sees their data

Every submission records the breeder's data-sharing choices. Raw genotype data are never released without the breeder's permission. Genotype visualizations, phenotype visualizations and exact GPS positions are seen by the project team only, unless the breeder also releases them to research collaborators and NNGA members.

Your choices at submission

When you submit trees you choose who may see each kind of data. The choice applies to every tree in that submission and is recorded with each tree in the project database. Project team only is the default for everything.

Who counts as each group

  • Project team: Rodale Institute and Savanna Institute staff working on this project.
  • Research collaborators & NNGA members: collaborating breeders and researchers, and current Northern Nut Growers Association members. They sign in on this website with an access code issued by the project team, and may also see the results in member reports, meetings and talks.
  • General public: anyone else visiting this website. They see submission records and general site locations only, never exact positions, genotype or phenotype results.

Raw genotype data

  • Raw DArTag genotype data for your trees are stored by Rodale Institute and used by the project team for the analyses described in the project.
  • Anyone else who wants the raw data for your trees must request it. The project team will ask you, and data are released only with your written permission.
  • Raw data are not posted on this website or in a public repository before publication without your permission.

How restricted data are protected

  • Restricted data are filtered on the project's server before anything is sent to a browser, based on the access code entered and on each tree's settings.
  • Trees set to Project team only are removed from plots and maps shown to collaborators and NNGA members.
  • Access codes are shared privately and can be changed by the project team at any time.
  • Uploaded spreadsheets and field maps are kept with a copy of each submission in a private project Google Drive folder, visible only to the project team. They are never placed on this public website.

What anyone can see on this site

To help balance sampling across programs, the website shows submission records to everyone: tree and submission IDs, program, maternal parent, species, material type, field name and position label, the general location of each site, and whether genotyping or phenotyping is in progress. No exact GPS positions, genotype results or phenotype results are shown to the general public.

What is never published

  • Submitter names and email addresses.
  • Raw genotype data, unless you give permission.
  • Visualizations and exact GPS positions to anyone outside the group you chose.

Changing your choices

You can change your choices at any time. Contact the project team with your program name and submission ID or batch ID, and the change will be applied to the affected trees.

This page summarizes the project's data-sharing practice for breeders. It is not a legal agreement; formal data-use terms can be arranged with the project team on request.
