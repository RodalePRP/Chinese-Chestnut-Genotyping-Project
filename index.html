Objectives and timeline

What the project will deliver

Commercial chestnut orchards trace back to a small number of breeding programs, and many selections are open-pollinated or lack pedigree records.

01

Large-scale DArTag genotype data

For diverse chestnut accessions, high-value cultivars and representative Castanea species.

02

Diversity, structure and relatedness

Population genomic analysis of the germplasm behind US commercial production.

03

Pedigrees and parentage

Reconstructed for high-value cultivars with incomplete or uncertain records.
